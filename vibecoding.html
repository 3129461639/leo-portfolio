一套用 Vibe Coding 完成的个人 IP 形象系统。以自己的照片为起点，和 AI 来回对话、反复调试提示词，最终得到一个稳定又可爱的「萌粒风」小人——从角色锚点和三视图出发，延展出头像、贴纸页和一整组表情包，让「我」在各个场景里都能被一眼认出。

A personal IP character system built through vibe coding. Starting from my own photos and iterating prompts back and forth with AI, a consistent "chibi" character emerged — from the anchor sheet and three-view turnaround, to avatars, sticker sheets and a full emoji pack. Instantly recognizable, everywhere.

把自己拍的照片 Vibe Coding 成一组海报。树冠间的天空、钟楼、林荫、水面的落叶、垂柳、人群与山海——先用镜头收集日常里的光，再和 AI 一起把它们重新排版、上色、写成海报。

A poster series vibe-coded from my own photographs — sky between treetops, a clock tower, summer canopy, leaves on water, willows, city crowds and the road to the sea. First collecting light with a camera, then re-composing it with AI into posters.

同一组海报练习的另一条线索：每一张海报都能找回它最初的那张照片。左边是按下快门的那一刻，右边是和 AI 反复对话后的再创作——古树的枝、池水的影、回廊的光、头顶的树冠、竹林的绿，一一对应。

The other thread of the same poster exercise: every poster traces back to an original photograph. On the left, the moment the shutter clicked; on the right, the reimagination after rounds of dialogue with AI — branches of an old tree, reflections on a pond, light in a corridor, the canopy overhead, the green of bamboo. One to one.

一段手势交互的录屏。用 Vibe Coding 做出的粒子文字效果——手指靠近时粒子被推开，划过时文字散开又聚拢，像水面被指尖拨动。代码和手感，都是和 AI 一轮轮聊出来的。

A screen recording of gesture interaction — a particle text effect built with vibe coding. Particles scatter as the finger approaches and regather as it passes, like ripples on water. Both the code and the feel were iterated through rounds of dialogue with AI.
