About Me · 关于我

Lü Weihao — a storyteller who dreams of creating ads that move people to tears. Trained in journalism and communication, now pursuing his Master's at Lanzhou University, his path runs through images and words: from shooting and cutting documentaries, to growing social accounts from zero to half a million monthly views at Mebo, to cold-starting four education accounts at Gaotu, to bringing a children's reading IP offline and into bookstores with Shusheng. He believes great advertising is both poetry and precision — emotion that moves people, and data that proves it. For him, a great future means facing everything on his own — and creating anyway.
